MORE ABOUT THIS HUMAN

This human is an INTJ, making him about as good in social situations as a chicken is at flying.

As a nerd with no social skills and a social battery that is permanently stuck on power-saving mode, the only things left to know about him are his favourite anime, movies, music, some random psychological and existential questions, and the things that keep him sleep-deprived.

This has resulted in what he proudly calls raccoon eyes, or what normal humans call dark circles.

But honestly, the more dramatic the better.
